# Campus Hostel Rules and Regulations (Academic Year 2026-2027)

## 1. General Conduct
- All residents must maintain discipline, academic decorum, and mutual respect within the hostel premises.
- Ragging or harassment of any form is strictly prohibited under zero-tolerance campus policy.
- Quiet study hours are observed daily from 22:00 to 06:00. Loud music or disruptive noise is prohibited during these hours.

## 2. Curfew and Biometric Entry
- The hostel main gates close at 22:30 on weekdays (Sunday through Thursday) and 23:00 on Fridays and Saturdays.
- Residents returning after curfew must produce valid warden authorization and log their entry with security.
- Biometric check-in is mandatory before 22:30.

## 3. Visitors and Guests
- Day visitors are permitted in designated ground-floor visitor lounges between 09:00 and 18:00 only.
- Overnight stay of non-residents or unauthorized guests is strictly forbidden.
- Violations incur a fine of 2,500 INR and disciplinary review.

## 4. Room Maintenance and Electrical Appliances
- High-power electrical appliances (electric heaters, induction cooktops, immersion coils) are prohibited in student rooms for fire safety.
- Room inventory and furniture must not be altered, removed, or painted.

## 5. Grievances and Medical Assistance
- Emergency medical desk: Health Center Ext. 4444 (24/7 on call).
- Hostel warden contact: warden-hostel@campus.edu (Office: Ground Floor, Block B).
